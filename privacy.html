私隱政策

兔寶協助你記錄產品、服務及相關文件。你輸入嘅記錄及匯入嘅文件儲存在裝置上。照片或文件辨識在裝置上處理；提醒使用系統本機通知。你可以在 App 中刪除記錄及管理附件。

較早版本遺留嘅水電帳單、用量、帳戶資料、原件或封存草稿如存在，亦屬裝置資料，並可能包含於完整備份。備份匯出至其他位置後，由你管理該副本。

當你主動分享或匯出，選定內容會交給你選擇嘅系統分享目的地。完整備份可由你選擇存到「檔案」或雲端位置；備份沒有額外密碼加密，請小心保管。備份還原會在確認後取代現有資料。應用鎖使用裝置系統認證，並不為匯出檔提供額外加密。

刪除記錄會從目前使用嘅資料庫移除相應記錄。為避免升級或還原失敗造成資料遺失，App 可能保留先前資料庫、升級安全副本或封存草稿；刪除目前記錄不會同步清除呢啲舊副本。完整刪除 App（唔係保留資料嘅卸載）會移除 App 容器內資料。你已匯出、分享或由系統／雲端另存嘅副本需在相關位置管理及刪除。如你透過電郵聯絡支援，電郵服務會處理郵件；你可要求刪除相關往來資料。

Privacy policy

Bunny Trove helps you record products, services, and related documents. Records and imported files are stored on your device. Text recognition is performed on the device, and reminders use local system notifications. You can delete records and manage attachments in the app.

Legacy utility bills, usage, account details, original documents, or archived drafts from earlier versions, if present, are also device data and may be included in complete backups. You control any backup copies exported elsewhere.

When you choose to share or export, selected content is passed to the system destination you choose. You can save a full backup to Files or a cloud location you select. Backups do not have additional password encryption; store them carefully. Restoring a backup replaces current data after confirmation. The optional app lock uses device authentication and does not add encryption to exported files.

Deleting a record removes it from the currently active database. To protect against failed upgrades or restores, the app may retain previous databases, upgrade safety copies or archived drafts; deleting a current record does not erase those retained copies. Deleting the app, rather than offloading it while keeping its data, removes its app-container data. Manage exported, shared, system-backup or cloud copies separately at their destinations. Your email service processes support messages; you may request deletion of support correspondence.
